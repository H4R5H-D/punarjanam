Ganga Aarti Experience

Evening lamps, chants and bells on the river.

As the light fades, lamps are raised to the Ganga with chants, bells and songs of devotion. Experiencing the aarti together is one of the most moving moments of a day in Rishikesh.

What to expect

  • Arrive before sunset to settle in.
  • Join the chanting, or simply watch.
  • A short walk by the river afterwards.
