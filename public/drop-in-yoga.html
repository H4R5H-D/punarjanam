What a session holds.

Each session moves gently through four parts, guided the whole way:

  • Asana. Simple movement to wake the body.
  • Pranayama. Breathing practices that steady the mind.
  • Meditation. A few quiet minutes of sitting still.
  • Relaxation. Lying down, doing nothing, letting it settle.

Traditional in spirit, beginner-friendly in practice. Rest whenever you need to.

Questions.

I've never done yoga. Can I join?

Yes. Most people who come have little or no experience. Every session is guided and you can rest whenever you like.

Can I join for just one day?

Yes. Drop-in sessions are made for people visiting Rishikesh for a day or two. Book a single session on WhatsApp.

What are the timings and prices?

Sessions are arranged around your plans, so timing and price depend on the format and group size. Message on WhatsApp and you'll get the next available session, the timing and the meeting point.

Do I need to bring a mat?

No. Mats and blankets are provided. Wear comfortable clothes and bring water.

Where do sessions happen?

By the Ganga in Rishikesh. Timings and the exact meeting point are arranged with you on WhatsApp or a quick call, around your plans.

Do you offer teacher training or certificates?

No. Punarjanma offers experiences, not courses or certifications.
