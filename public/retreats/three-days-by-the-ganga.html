Three days by the Ganga

A short, unhurried pause for people who don't have long.

Length
3 days, 2 nights
Group
Up to 10 people
Price
Shared on enquiry
Where
Rishikesh

The retreat.

Three days are enough to notice a different rhythm. Mornings begin at the river, days move slowly, and evenings end with kirtan. There's time to practise and time to do nothing at all.

Day by day.

  1. Arrive and settle

    • Arrive by early afternoon and settle into your room
    • Gentle evening practice by the river
    • Shared vegetarian dinner
    • Kirtan to close the day

  2. River, fire and forest

    • Early morning Ganga dip (optional)
    • Morning yoga and pranayama
    • Havan with a local pandit
    • Afternoon nature walk
    • Free evening to explore

  3. Closing

    • Sunrise meditation
    • Morning practice
    • Closing circle
    • Lunch together and departure

Where you'll stay.

A simple, clean guesthouse a short walk from the river, with shared or private rooms, hot water and a quiet garden. Comfortable, not luxurious.

Included

  • 2 nights' accommodation
  • All vegetarian meals
  • All yoga sessions and meditations
  • Havan and kirtan
  • Guided nature walk
  • Mats and props

Not included

  • Travel to and from Rishikesh
  • Personal expenses
  • Travel insurance
