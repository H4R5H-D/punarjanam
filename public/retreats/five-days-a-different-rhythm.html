The retreat.

Five days give you room to settle in. Alongside daily practice there's the river, fire ritual, music, long walks and slow meals together, with afternoons left open for you.

Day by day.

  1. Arrive and settle

    • Arrival and welcome
    • Gentle evening practice
    • Shared dinner

  2. The river

    • Early morning Ganga dip (optional)
    • Morning yoga and pranayama
    • Afternoon at leisure
    • Evening meditation

  3. Fire and music

    • Morning practice
    • Havan with a local pandit
    • Walk through old Rishikesh
    • Evening kirtan

  4. Into the hills

    • Sunrise practice
    • Half-day nature walk with a packed lunch
    • Restorative evening session

  5. Closing

    • Final morning practice
    • Closing circle
    • Lunch together and departure

Where you'll stay.

A simple, comfortable guesthouse near the Ganga. Shared and private rooms available.
