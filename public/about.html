Founder, Punarjanam

Meet Mansi

I'm Mansi. My journey with yoga gradually became a deeper exploration of life and the self, shaped by years of practice, sadhana, time in an ashram and living close to the Ganga in Rishikesh.

Punarjanam was born from a desire to share a little of what this journey has allowed me to experience the essence of yoga beyond the mat, spirituality beyond what we simply read or hear about, and the profound beauty in experiences we often take for granted.

Through yoga, retreats and thoughtfully curated spiritual experiences, I hope to offer people a glimpse of something they may not have encountered before something that perhaps cannot be fully explained, but can only be felt.

I don't promise a transformation or prescribe a path. I simply wish to create experiences that may touch someone's life in ways words cannot express. Where that experience takes them is theirs to discover.

What Punarjanam is.

  • Drop-in yoga for travellers and beginners
  • Signature three- and five-day retreats
  • Spiritual experiences by the Ganga
  • Time and space, not a programme

What it isn't.

  • A teacher training school
  • A certificate or diploma
  • A luxury resort or spa
  • A promise of transformation

Come as you are.
