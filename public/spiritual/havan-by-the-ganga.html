Havan by the Ganga

A sacred fire ritual beside the river.

A havan is a traditional fire ritual. Seated around the sacred fire, participants offer herbs, grains and ghee into the flames with each mantra, as an act of gratitude and intention.

What to expect

  • Seated around the fire with the group.
  • Each offering made with a mantra.
  • A quiet, grounding close.
