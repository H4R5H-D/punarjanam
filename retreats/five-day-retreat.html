What's included in the experience.

  1. Deep Noble Silence Experience

    A dedicated day of Noble Silence supported by thoughtfully designed silence-based activities to reduce external distractions and encourage deeper self-observation.

  2. Daily Morning Yoga

    Progressive guided sessions combining yoga asanas, pranayama and meditation.

  3. Ganga Connection

    Mindful Ganga dips and moments of stillness by the river.

  4. Five Elements Cleansing

    Meditative experiences inspired by the five elements.

  5. Deeper Pranayama Practices

    Explore different breathing techniques and develop greater breath awareness.

  6. Guided Inner Reflection

    Experiential activities, journaling and self-observation.

  7. Spiritual & Devotional Experiences

    Bhakti Kirtan, Hanuman Chalisa, Havan and traditional rituals.

  8. Nature Connection & Grounding

    Mindful outdoor experiences amidst greenery and natural surroundings.

  9. Special Full-Day Spiritual Excursion

    Visit spiritually significant and peaceful high-vibrational places around Rishikesh and its surroundings. This excursion combines sacred spaces, nature exploration, local experiences and the joy of travelling together.

  10. Seva Experience

    Participate in meaningful acts of service and compassion.

  11. Ganga Aarti

    Experience the spiritual atmosphere of the evening Ganga Aarti.

  12. Closing & Sharing Circle

    Conclude the retreat with collective reflection, experience sharing and gratitude.

The Experience

Five immersive days combining silence, yoga, spirituality, nature and exploration, offering a deeper opportunity to disconnect from everyday routines and reconnect with yourself.

Common to both retreats.

  • Comfortable retreat accommodation.
  • Two nutritious vegetarian meals daily (brunch and dinner).
  • Daily yoga, pranayama and meditation.
  • Five-elements cleansing practices.
  • Spiritual and nature-based experiences.
  • Dedicated time for rest and personal exploration.
