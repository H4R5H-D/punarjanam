Women's circle

A small retreat for women, held gently.

Length
3 days, 2 nights
Group
Up to 8 women

Shared on enquiry

The retreat.

A quiet, small-group retreat for women, with gentle practice, the river, shared meals and conversation. Dates are set when a group comes together, so tell us when you'd like to come.

Day by day.

  1. Arrive

    • Welcome and opening circle
    • Gentle evening practice
    • Dinner together

  2. Together

    • Morning practice by the Ganga
    • Nature walk
    • Havan
    • Evening kirtan

  3. Closing

    • Morning meditation
    • Closing circle
    • Lunch and departure

Where you'll stay.

A simple guesthouse near the river, women-only floor.

Included

  • 2 nights' accommodation
  • All vegetarian meals
  • All sessions and experiences

Not included

  • Travel to and from Rishikesh
  • Personal expenses
