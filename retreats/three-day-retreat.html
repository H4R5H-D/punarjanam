What's included in the experience.

  1. Daily Morning Yoga

    Progressive sessions combining yoga asanas, pranayama and meditation.

  2. Three Consecutive Ganga Dips

    A mindful morning connection with the sacred Ganga.

  3. Pranayama & Breathwork

    Guided breathing practices to cultivate awareness and inner stillness.

  4. Five Elements Cleansing

    Meditative experiences inspired by Earth, Water, Fire, Air and Space.

  5. Nature Walk & Grounding

    Mindful walking and connecting with the natural surroundings.

  6. Energetic Bhakti Kirtan

    Hanuman Chalisa, devotional singing, clapping and dancing.

  7. Havan & Traditional Rituals

    An experience of devotion, gratitude and traditional spiritual practices.

  8. Seva Experience

    Meaningful acts of service and compassion.

  9. Ganga Aarti

    Experience the beauty and spiritual atmosphere of the evening Ganga Aarti.

  10. Closing Circle

    Collective experience sharing, reflection and gratitude.

The Experience

Three days of yoga, breathwork, nature, devotion and conscious living, creating space to slow down and reconnect.

Common to both retreats.

  • Comfortable retreat accommodation.
  • Two nutritious vegetarian meals daily (brunch and dinner).
  • Daily yoga, pranayama and meditation.
  • Five-elements cleansing practices.
  • Spiritual and nature-based experiences.
  • Dedicated time for rest and personal exploration.
